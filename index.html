How learning works here

  1. 1

    Pick a path

    Each path is an ordered route from fundamentals to production. Every lesson lists what it needs first.

  2. 2

    Read the lesson

    Plain-language explanation, diagrams and a worked example you can reproduce, written here on the site.

  3. 3

    See it in the wild

    Four or five documented uses in real products, each with its problem, steps, tradeoff and sources.

  4. 4

    Practise, earn XP

    Do the exercise, check the solution, mark the lesson complete and build the capstone at the end.
